RPH TRACKER VERSION HISTORY

v1.5 - Initialization

First functional iteration, group scanning and basic calibration.

--------------------------------
Refactored refresh vs rebuild logic.
Improved PARK safety and group caching.

--------------------------
Added per-LCD configuration, sorting and display modes.
Introduced debug and compact layouts.

- Global calibration implemented (calibrate_all)
- Refresh/rebuild_cd separation finalized, PARK-safe confirmed
- Alphabetical subgroup sorting verified functional
- Pending features: per-part calibration, velocity readout, configurable update interval
